# C1901 Introduction

![C1901 Banner](/img/c1901-banner.png)

## Introduction

The Jetson Orin Nano Lite **(C1901)** is a cost-effective carrier board developed for the **Jetson Orin Nano & NX** modules. Based on the reference design, it trims and optimizes part of the reference functionality — without compromising the basic features — to greatly improve cost efficiency and help customers cut costs while boosting productivity.

## Product Features

- Developed from the reference design, offering an experience close to the official board
- Supports Orin Nano 4G/8G & Orin NX 8G/16G
- High-quality 6-layer ENIG PCB, all-MLCC capacitors and gold-plated connectors
- Supports NVME SSD and M.2 network cards

| V1.2 | 2024 Q2 | Added RTC battery connector |

> - This board supports 12V (9V~19V) power input; make sure the power supply provides sufficient wattage.
> - The power connector is DC5521, compatible with the DC5525 plug.
> - When the supply voltage exceeds 15V under heavy load, please improve cooling.
> - This product supports reverse-polarity protection on the power input.
> - Each USB group outputs up to 2.5A total.
> - Compared with the reference board, the rear network card and M.2 2230 interfaces are laid out symmetrically (mirrored).

## Notes on Selected Features

- Under certain power loads the board may emit a faint whine caused by the inductors; this does not affect normal operation.
- The HDMI port is adapted from a DP port and only supports 1080P resolution under the reference firmware.
- The 5V output on the 40-pin GPIO header has back-feed protection, with a maximum current of 0.7A.
- The SSD must be a single-sided version; double-sided versions may interfere and cause short circuits.
- The RTC battery connector is MX1.25-2P, battery voltage 3V — do not reverse the polarity.
