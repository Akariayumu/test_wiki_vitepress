# C2402 Introduction

::: warning Pre-release Documentation
This page is a pre-release preview of the C2402 carrier board. Certain interface details and mechanical drawings are pending final verification and remain subject to the official production release documentation.
:::

![C2402 Banner](/img/c2402-banner.webp)

## Overview

The **C2402** is a highly integrated, compact carrier board developed for **Jetson Orin Nano / Orin NX** modules. Within a small footprint, it delivers a complete set of machine-vision and industrial communication interfaces, including **dual Gigabit Ethernet ports**, **four USB 3.2 Type-A ports (5Gbps)**, a **GMSL2 camera expansion connector**, and onboard expansion for **4G/5G cellular modules** and **M.2 2280 NVMe SSDs**.

---

## Features

- Equipped with an automotive-grade **E-fuse** protection IC providing over-voltage, under-voltage, short-circuit, over-current, and reverse-polarity protection
- Ultra-low-resistance Buck converters paired with flat-wire alloy inductors for low heat generation and high saturation current
- High-quality **8-layer, 2 μin ENIG via-in-pad PCB** in a compact form factor
- **Four USB 3.2 Type-A ports (5Gbps)**, **two 4-pin GH1.25 USB 2.0 connectors**, and **dual Gigabit RJ45 Ethernet ports** (RTL8111 + Intel I210)
- Dedicated GMSL camera expansion connector (supports up to **4 × GMSL2 cameras** via an adapter board) plus an onboard M.2 Key B slot with a front Nano SIM slot for 4G/5G cellular connectivity
- Same-side (rear) M.2 Key M, Key B, and Key E layout — swap the wireless card, 4G/5G module, or NVMe SSD without removing the Jetson module

| Storage | 1 × M.2 Key M (2280, NVMe SSD) |

| USB-C | 1 × USB Type-C (for flashing firmware) |
| Camera | 1 × GMSL camera expansion connector (supports up to 4 × GMSL2 cameras via adapter board) |
| Other interfaces | UART, CAN, I²C, SPI, GPIO |
| RTC battery | Supported, 2-pin backup battery connector, 3V |
| PoE | Not supported |
| Operating temperature | 0 °C to 45 °C |

::: warning Notes
- A 12V power supply is recommended (10V–30V input supported; under-voltage cutoff at 9.5V, over-voltage cutoff at 31V). Improve cooling under heavy load when the input voltage exceeds 15V.
- Reverse-polarity protection is provided on the power input, but never connect power in reverse.
- Each USB port group supplies up to 2.5A.
- **Only insert or remove the Jetson module when the carrier board is powered off.**
- Single-sided NVMe SSDs are recommended to prevent mechanical interference or short-circuiting.
:::

## Selected Functions and Interfaces

### Power Connector (3)

Uses an **XT30 (2+2) PW-M** hybrid connector (two main power contacts plus two auxiliary CAN signal pins). The recommended input voltage is **12V** (**10V–30V** supported; under-voltage protection threshold **9.5V**, over-voltage protection threshold **31V**).

| Module | Recommended Power Supply | USB-C PD Requirement (via PD-to-XT30 Trigger Cable) |
|--------|--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Jetson Orin Nano 4GB / 8GB | ≥ 45W | 20V 2.25A |
| Jetson Orin NX 8GB / 16GB | ≥ 60W | 20V 3A |

::: warning Power Input Notice
**The onboard USB Type-C port (No. 7) is strictly for flashing firmware and does NOT accept power input.** The `USB-C PD Requirement` column above applies **only** when powering the board through the **XT30 power connector (No. 3)** using an external **USB-C PD to XT30 trigger adapter cable**. Detailed CAN pin polarity on the XT30 (2+2) connector will be provided in the final release documentation.
:::

### Power / REC Buttons and Function DIP Switch (1, 2, 23)

- **Enter recovery mode:** With power disconnected, hold the `REC` button while applying power (recovery mode lasts ~60 seconds and boots normally if no host connection is detected); alternatively, set **DIP switch 3** on the back to `ON` to enter recovery mode automatically on every power-up.
- **Power button:** While running, press once to open the shutdown dialog or hold for 10 seconds to force power-off; while powered off, press once to power on.
- **Disable auto power-on:** Set **DIP switch 2** on the back to `ON` to disable automatic power-on.
- **CAN termination resistor:** Set **DIP switch 1** on the back to `ON` to enable the onboard 120Ω CAN bus termination resistor.

![C2402 function DIP switch and RTC connector](/img/c2402-switch.webp)

*Rear 4-position function DIP switch and RTC battery connector*

| Position | Silkscreen | Function When `ON` | Default |
|----------|------------|--------------------|---------|
| 1 | `CAN 120R` | Enables onboard 120Ω CAN bus termination resistor | `OFF` (disabled) |
| 2 | `AUTO ON DIS` | Disables auto power-on when power is applied (press Power button to boot) | `OFF` (auto power-on) |
| 3 | `REC` | Automatically enters Force Recovery mode on every power-on | `OFF` (normal boot) |
| 4 | — | Reserved | `OFF` |

::: warning Reset DIP Switch 3 After Flashing
If you set DIP switch 3 (`REC`) to `ON` to enter recovery mode for flashing, **return it to `OFF` once the host detects the board or before rebooting**, otherwise the system will re-enter recovery mode on reboot.
:::

### GH1.25 Peripheral Connectors

Multiple peripheral interfaces are brought out along the upper edge of the front side via GH1.25 connectors (No. `11–18` in the front layout diagram). **Viewing each connector from the orientation of the front layout diagram, pins are numbered from left to right (Pin 1 to Pin 6):**

| No. | Interface | Connector | Pin 1 | Pin 2 | Pin 3 | Pin 4 | Pin 5 | Pin 6 |
|-----|-----------|-----------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| 11 | SPI0 | GH1.25-6P | GND | CS0 | MOSI | MISO | SCK | 5V |
| 12 | USB 2.0-1 | GH1.25-4P | GND | D+ | D− | 5V | — | — |
| 13 | USB 2.0-2 | GH1.25-4P | GND | D+ | D− | 5V | — | — |
| 14 | GPIO | GH1.25-4P | GND | GPIO13 | GPIO7 | 5V | — | — |
| 15 | UART1 | GH1.25-4P | GND | RX | TX | 5V | — | — |
| 16 | UART2 DEBUG | GH1.25-4P | GND | RX | TX | 5V | — | — |
| 17 | I²C1 | GH1.25-4P | GND | SDA | SCL | 5V | — | — |
| 18 | CAN0 | GH1.25-2P | CAN_L | CAN_H | — | — | — | — |

::: warning Signal Logic Level Warning
- All **I²C, GPIO, SPI, and UART signal pins** on the GH1.25 connectors use **3.3V logic levels** (the `5V` pin on each connector is a peripheral power output only). Never drive 5V logic signals directly into these pins without level shifting, as doing so will damage the Jetson module!
- `CAN0` integrates an onboard CAN transceiver. For software configuration and testing, see [CAN Bus](/en/orin-nano-series/peripherals#_4-can-bus).
:::

### GMSL Camera Expansion Connector (19)

A high-speed board-to-board camera expansion connector (No. 19) on the front side supports up to **4 × GMSL2 cameras** simultaneously when paired with a dedicated GMSL2 deserializer adapter board.

![C2402 with 4-channel GMSL2 camera adapter board](/img/c2402-gmsl.webp)

*C2402 with 4-channel GMSL2 camera adapter board installed*

### 4G/5G Cellular Module Interface (20, 24)

The rear side provides an **M.2 Key B** slot (No. 24, supporting 2230 / 3042 cellular modules), paired with a **Nano SIM card slot** on the front side (No. 20, silkscreen `SIM1`). For driver setup and dial-up configuration, see [4G Cellular Module Configuration](/en/orin-nano-series/4g-module).

### RJ45 Ethernet Ports (8)

Both ports in the stacked RJ45 receptacle are Gigabit Ethernet interfaces, powered respectively by **RTL8111** and industrial-grade **Intel I210** Ethernet controllers (upper/lower port assignment will be finalized in the official release documentation).

### M.2 Slots and RTC Battery Connector (21, 22, 24, 25)

- **Same-side M.2 layout:** The M.2 Key M (2280 NVMe SSD), M.2 Key B (4G/5G cellular module), and M.2 Key E (2230 wireless card) slots are all located on the back of the carrier board, allowing storage or wireless module upgrades without removing the Jetson module.
- **RTC backup battery connector (22):** Located next to the rear DIP switch (2-pin, 3V). Check the `+` polarity silkscreen on the PCB before connecting and **never reverse the polarity**. For RTC software configuration, see [RTC Interface](/en/orin-nano-series/peripherals#_5-rtc-interface).
